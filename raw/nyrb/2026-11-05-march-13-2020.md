> Because the islands bright white          solid stone  
>  Reach down through shifting mud beneath the creek  
>  Except what might be seen          by radar meek  
>  Orisons sounding the meek’s due          but none  
>  Near for use          things lost in their pocks are gone  
>  Never to be regained          I lost a week  
>  Ago an action          figure’s golden beak-  
>  Shaped mask in the dark          and I’ve come back alone
>
> Today again to search the plunging edge  
>  As I have come each day          and stood unwilling  
>  Yesterday and before afraid and stand  
>  Looking at the dark water          who will dredge  
>  Open the bed          and risk the vipers and  
>  Refresh the toy with which I practice killing
