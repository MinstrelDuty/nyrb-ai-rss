# 正文

> No, no, I don’t believe in my poems,  
> Not really, but I defer to the poet in me  
> Who belies belief with a fact that  
> Requires no belief, to write a poem  
> In which fire is the poetic  
> Element, fire burning in a wood  
> At night, the leaves falling in flames  
> From trees, and he lets this poem mean  
> All that poetry infers but does not say  
> Because, to him, a poem doesn’t say  
> All that it means but keeps the silent  
> Stillness of a wood after the fire dies,  
> And smoke rising from the ash.
