# 正文

> My father brought books to the basement.   
> During the air raids we read   
> *Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea*, *Treasure Island*,   
> *Around the World in Eighty Days*,   
> and ate feasts my mother made with dry goods and powdered milk:   
> rice pudding, “béchamel” spaghetti,   
> in one large aluminum pot over a small gas burner,   
> sandwiches of Picon cheese   
> she cut in triangles, like a tea party.   
> We had tea party   
> picnics   
> in the basement   
> or on the small patch of green   
> by the road.   
> And after reading *Aladdin*, I spent a day sitting   
> on the carpet in the living room   
> cross-legged, clutching the edges and hoping.
