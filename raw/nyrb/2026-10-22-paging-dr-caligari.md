# 正文

> It’s not vertigo exactly, more a diagonal feeling  
> as if a giant had picked up the room and were  
> about to spill everything, you included.
>
> And then there is the tired-as-Sisyphus feeling  
> from drawing a load of shadows up a painted hill.  
> A film of spirits clouds our eyes, making it impossible  
> to watch the movie. Don’t tell me what happens next.
>
> Can a person already in a trance be hypnotized again?  
> I need to decide something—anything will do.  
> It looks about to rain, but I’m going for a walk  
> to exercise what’s left of my free will.
