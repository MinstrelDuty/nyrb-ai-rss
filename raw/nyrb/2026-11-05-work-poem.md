# 正文

> I’m too broke to make the work   
> Or I don’t care enough about the work   
> To be as broke as I should let myself be   
> To have the time to make the work   
> I work too hard to make the work   
> Or my indolence is too great to take that pleasure   
> In the work that the work needs   
> Or once I arrive as a boat from sea   
> At the limits of my intelligence   
> I quail and put the work away   
> I do not push the work or   
> Push the work too hard   
> So the work becomes working   
> A tough dough, not play   
> That I want to have fun, to see   
> The delights of the world   
> With my eyes and drink wine and say   
> This is the work of the work   
> Let it relinquish me, tame   
> Monster, to roam freely among the delights of the world   
> That yet I travel too lightly, quaffing   
> The brine of forgetfulness, that I should stay   
> In some hermitage and let the work of the world   
> Be, for it circles fine   
> Without me, that the medium   
> Of the work is inauthentic and circumscribes   
> The world of works in false conceit,   
> Belt that begets its own limitations, that   
> I speak these words I call my ideas   
> But lack their commitments   
> That, gadfly I, delight to say   
> I am working on   
> I am working on, and the work   
> A boat tossed at sea   
> Never arrives.
>
> No, these are not paradisiacal thoughts.   
> They do not befit   
> This situation, which is that of the garnet   
> In the anguish, the anguish   
> In the garnet, the cities   
> In the anguish, in the garnet   
> All correspondence, all array   
> And its open, raw, unlikely vitality.
