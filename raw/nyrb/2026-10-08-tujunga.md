# 正文

> As blood is brought where infection is   
> So was I brought to Los Angeles
>
> Toting only my REI membership card   
> And University of California ID
>
> “Access the inaccessible”   
> My headlamp quibbled
>
> But I didn’t need its LEDs   
> To see them, the coyotes,
>
> Rip the heads from Griffin’s chickens   
> In broad daylight when I’d let them out to graze
>
> Because Griffin told me to because   
> He’d made a calculation
>
> Their freedom of movement   
> Was worth the risk of death, certain
>
> Though it seemed to me, even when the coyotes   
> Left the necks intact, bleeding
>
> And I had Mike come over and finish the job   
> And I just watched and it was and was not
>
> Like the news, it wasn’t like watching the news   
> At all, but it helped me understand the news
