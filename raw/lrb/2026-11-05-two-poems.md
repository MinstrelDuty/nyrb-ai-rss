# 正文

### Sometimes We Are in the Same City

After Rilke/After Tsvetaeva

Half a life half my life a half-life  
were/are you?

Such immediate/such fitting  
recognition

we floated into place. What next/  
what more?

Absence contrived within and later of  
ourselves

as invitation/emptiness only  
the other

could unlock/furnish/complete.  
What we met

in each impossible room  
was love not compensation.

We were already full/already  
wrong/already

impossible to contain  
and we could not stop building.

How to contain/how to be contained  
by so many rooms

when what we met could not teach us  
how to be/be still/

still be/the missing/the impossible/  
more/still?

### How It Holds

For Garry Fabian Miller

Last year we each arrived differently  
– me suddenly, you slowly –

there on the edge of things  
where all we are is waiting.

You had been directed  
by increments to listen, to decide.

I was without  
time or space and all decided for me

in the far dark  
where light takes hold of light

and re-completes to black and  
here we are:

each next de-completion  
a going on.

What else is life? Interrupting  
into colour and all that colour allows,

deflects, floods and draws in,  
how it holds and forms.
