# 正文

Hoopoe is the thing  
With feathers, and a crest,  
Or else *a* thing with feathers,  
Striped wings, and tawny breast.  
  
\*  
  
(I never saw a hoopoe –  
Unless I was alone –  
Except some interloper  
Took out their telephone.)  
  
\*  
  
Because I could not stop  
To watch the hoopoe, he  
Made his gentle Poe-Poe-Poe  
Of incredulity.  
  
\*  
  
Maybe it’s a loophole –  
Or maybe I’m a dope:  
I never met a hoopoe  
And did not think of ‘hope’.
