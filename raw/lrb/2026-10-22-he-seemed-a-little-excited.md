# 正文

Beyond the range of the dog, I knew there were snaky corridors at Meara’s, as well as a door that leads to a garden with the big round pond, which is a big surprise in summer, presenting its challenge: *So will you do something? – with me?*

Earlier at her party Simon Shaw had seemed a little excited when I met up with him.

Was that sexual interest? – but what did Meara think? I thought she would know and I asked her.

But while saying nothing, Meara took me hard by the arm and led me to her front door, which she opened.

Soon I was on my own, chest high in warm water in my bathtub.

\*

‘How do you like the cold?’ Simon had asked me at the party.

‘I like it,’ I had said.

‘And the heat?’ he had asked.

But I prefer to think about Veronica, the dog, whose body levitates on her little feet while trotting.

Hasn’t the dog got everything someone should have? – peace of mind and pride, surely. What else?

\*

A window screen wobbled in the high wind, with a life pulse, and briefly stalled my thoughts – that Meara might have been my long-gone mother, the closest thing to, but so has ended another association with a single figure who can guide me.

In their place is my cat Peridot, who came up to the tub and who said, of course, nothing to me over and over.

Lately she has been in good spirits off and on with wild eyes and raised hackles – *Let’s go in! Let’s get out! Go in! Charge off!*

I do not know how all this ends. I am not supposed to.

\*

Wednesday I was visiting my friend Iola, who was in a hurry to finish up with the eggs she was beating when her little girl interrupted.

With her eggy fork in the air, Iola said, ‘Oh, don’t cry, Jane! And say you’re sorry!’

Instead, the little girl said, ‘*Is she staying?*’ Iola kept up with her beating and stuck her tongue out at the eggs, but then she added minced items and cheese. She filled a mould, left rice on the burner to boil, while Jane approached the table angry.

You could say Iola’s food is nourishing and attractive – you could even say it is lovable in flavour, and that not one of us – Iola, Jane or me – has anything like equivalent appeal.
