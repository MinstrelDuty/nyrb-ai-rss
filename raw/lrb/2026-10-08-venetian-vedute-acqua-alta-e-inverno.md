# 正文

In storm, the rain drives hard into the choppy canals –  
churning them white against this glittering grey.  
The lagoon, no longer black and flat, seems part of the rain  
and splashes back all day, until – in the mussel-light –  
all water seems to have moved from flat to vertical,  
passing up and down through the slate-blue, porous dusk.

\*

After the long rains, the autumn auguries of flood:  
the air deepens, seems almost to stop, so you hear the waves  
breaking, out on the rocks of Malamocco.  
The canal-water has stilled, in sympathy  
as it senses the sirocco riding the rising tide, and the surface shivers  
and the depths green through, as the sea-water flushes in.

\*

San Marco is the first to go under the darkening sky: the waves  
beating at the boats on the Riva, splintering them, whelming the stone  
and spilling over, flooding under the arches of the Palazzo Ducale,  
welling beneath the city, under the paving, the whole lagoon  
bubbling up through the slabs of the Piazza, turning the storm-drains  
so they surge as fountains, till the spouts are all swallowed and drowned.

\*

Some canals were blocked by barges – lodged tight under the bridges  
by driving water – while rowboats move through the narrow streets  
and those gondolas not smashed at their moorings  
are out in the Piazza gliding past Florian’s, dodging its chairs  
and tables that are all drifting away, among the ghosts of napkins,  
bills of fare, newspapers, tablecloths; a pale dog, swimming.

\*

Through the gardens of Castello, trees stand in their gold: the gowns,  
the trains of their fallen leaves. In the canals, the small fish drift  
like tinsel; a jellyfish goes sculling past. The boats start to chafe  
against their ropes, tug at their mooring-poles, griding  
hard against the quay, as a wind lifts from the east  
and the birch shivers off the last of its leaves. And then it is winter.

\*

The time it takes for a firework to hang its colour on the black heights  
and die. This is painting with light, and time stops. You see nothing else.  
Darts of twining arrows shiver into the night, with a crump and frizzle,  
blowing open into burst necklaces, which then disappear –  
melting like snow to leave nothing but after-images, ghosts: light-trails  
seared on the eye, the absence of sound. Smoke on a battlefield.

\*

The lagoon’s flat blackness like an oilcloth. The city  
scarfed in fog, seen through this scrim of light,  
is closing round its hints and rumours, its muted sounds:  
the slosh and slop of wash from the boats,  
the moan of wind through the stone canals.  
Winter narrowing. The whole sky stitched with stars.

\*

A barbed wind whipped in across the lagoon, straight  
from the Carpathians, bringing winter. Torches cut the darkness;  
boats moving through, with the pass of a blade.  
Fat flakes of snow are falling fast; the city soon  
a negative of itself: a white city standing on black water;  
the whiteness flawed by lines of forked footprints, a pattern of paws.
