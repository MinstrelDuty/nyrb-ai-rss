# 正文

### My Process

For J.A.

You thought that to leave  
everything out –

not try to explain  
what the world doesn’t –

might be a form  
of innocence –

Venus as Virgin  
standing on a clam shell –

why not?

And, if fault was  
to be found,

you’d leave it  
to the beachcombers.

Of course, I’m speaking  
of myself, not you.

                \*

My process  
is skipping

a rock  
across water.

### Cascade

Since thoughts  
are anonymous,

we might do well  
to treat them with caution;

since they appear  
fully formed

like gods,

like toadstools  
on a wet lawn –

like this small pink fairy  
‘ink-cap’

alone  
on a dissolving plank.
