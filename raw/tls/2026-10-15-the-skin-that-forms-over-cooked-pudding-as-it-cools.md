# The skin that forms over cooked pudding as it cools,

October 16, 2026

the worn-down soles of long-worn shoes,  
that attenuated.  
Finespun as spun sugar.  
As the walls of a silk cocoon.  
Not unbeautiful.

Theirs is the flimsy beauty  
of starlets on old celluloid that burns  
in the projector.  
Thus are the souls of the long-dead  
when I think on them.

When I think on them, they seem  
almost trivial, the ones I loved,  
and even the unlovable ones.  
Lite, in the marketing sense.  
Diminished in fat and flavour.

Like teenagers who don’t read,  
who attend church but aren’t afraid  
of Jesus.  
Lifetime members of the Poetry Club,  
cavorting at beach parties.
