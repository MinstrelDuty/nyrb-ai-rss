went hunting in the orchard with the baker and the priest _An orchardful of apples and the raven in his nest_

The priest forsook his Bible and the baker left his yeast _An orchardful of apples and the raven in his nest_

The soldier lent his rifle to a bailiff at the jail _An orchardful of apples and the raven in his nest_

They hoped to catch their lunch with holly birdlime and a pail _\_An orchardful of apples and the raven in his nest\__

_Shane McCrae’s most recent book is_ New and Collected Hell _, 2025. He lives in New York and teaches at Columbia University._
