# The Importance of Time

![Image 2](https://www.the-tls.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/6414.jpg?w=116)

September 18, 2026

[Read this issue](https://www.the-tls.com/issues/current-issue)

The ghosts wear watches –  
ironically. When asked the time  
they reply “Eternity!”  
as did the poet Batyushkov  
who for that earned the odium of Mandelstam.

Quite a row of them on the old park bench  
under an always waning moon  
trying out ever more tortuous tenses  
havering between  
pluperfect and passato remoto.

Should one of their number be so  
coarse as to mention the present  
there follows a flurry of nightgowns,  
a clearing of throats.  
Then an older, timeworn ghost

– the exact same age as the others –  
might point at the sky  
where a barn owl has passed  
and bequeathed an augury with feathers  
to regale the uncertain hour.****

**JAMIE McKENDRICK**
