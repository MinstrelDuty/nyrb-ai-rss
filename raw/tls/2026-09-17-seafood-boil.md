# Seafood boil

September 18, 2026

[Read this issue](https://www.the-tls.com/issues/current-issue)

March in St Augustine,  
canopy roof plump with medlar fruit  
and blet persimmon, the trees humming  
while crawdad tails fanned out under chrome nails  
and non-verbal children in floral dungarees  
chucked wheat bags at holes in the ground,  
others resting their faces  
on the lip of the piano’s white teeth  
in that house perfumed by fried okra,  
by what they call “_hot kay-soh_”,  
and Marina’s chocolate lava cakes.  
While considering geographical impermanence  
and Google’s supercomputer  
we prised 200 sweet meats from their shells,  
stumped occasionally by crab legs locked at right angles,  
those barbed bodies filched from Louisiana waters.  
When the bell tolled, hot buckets fell  
with paprika, earthy starch, neon corn kernels  
slotting below pink meat as Labradors lollygagged  
and potatoes thumped, chilli-peppered,  
soon dunked in vats of coagulating butter.  
Here I learned the twist of a crawfish,  
to pinch the head and suck out the orange matter,  
to try new things with gusto  
and to relax around the pregnant trees,  
the deep-voiced pilsner veterans  
resting in their shade.

**EMMELINE ARMITAGE**

_Emmeline Armitage is a writer and musician from West Yorkshire. Her work has appeared in Wonderland and the London Magazine. She is the host of the culture and literature podcast Out-of-Print._
