## Toby Lichtig and Lucy Dallas talk to M. John Harrison, the winner of this year’s Booker Prize, about _The End of Everything_ and his life in books

Toby Lichtig and Lucy Dallas talk to M. John Harrison, the winner of this year’s Booker Prize, about _The End of Everything_ and his life in books. They discuss literary criticism, alien invasion, the tyranny of genre and being given “permission” to write at the age of eighty-one.
